The land deal.

The sequence

The deal in five moves

Each move was legal on paper. Together they removed almost every check.

Move 1 · 2023Set up the vehicle

Atlantic Incubation Partners LLC registers in Delaware. A company of the same name later appears in Abu Dhabi.

Move 2 · Feb 2024Change the law

Law 21/2024 rewrites protected-area rules. Critics say it lets the PM override protections.

Move 3 · Oct 2024Partner up

Kushner and Alabbar become directors of an Abu Dhabi holding company, co-owned with Eagle Hills.

Move 4 · Dec 2024Fast-track

"Strategic investor" status for Sazan, reportedly without a business plan.

Move 5 · 2025–26Buy land, bring bulldozers

A new Vlorë land company appears. By May 2026, satellites show roads and clearing without a published permit.

Who owns the Zvërnec coast?

Each layer is a different answer, from the outside story down to the registry records. Press Peel the next layer to go one level deeper.

1 · What the public seesPress / NGO report
Fences, bulldozers and a brand name: "Sazan Real Estate Development", named in a PR statement as managing the project. AFP found no legal trace of a company with that name.
2 · What the government saysPress / NGO report
The Zvërnec investors are "the two co-owners of Power International Holding W.L.L from Qatar", and the land is private property.
3 · The legal shareholderOfficial record
Albania Land Development shpk, Vlorë (NIPT M56727208I, registered 21 May 2025). Its registered shareholder and administrator is one local individual, listed at 100%.
4 · The beneficial ownersOfficial record
Albania's beneficial-owner register lists Ramez Alkhayyat and Mohamadmoataz Alkhayat, 50% each, with ties to Albania and Qatar. Sayari shows no other holdings for either.
5 · What investigators are looking atAllegation
BIRN reports that ultimate ownership was routed through a Dutch trust. Reporting says prosecutors froze the company's accounts over allegedly fraudulent land titles, with prices inflated 22-fold. None of this has been proven in court.

Still unknown

What the records can't answer yet

  • Which company actually holds the Sazan rights inside Albania? No Albanian company tied to Atlantic Incubation Partners appears in the records.
  • Are the three companies called Atlantic Incubation Partners (Delaware, Florida, Abu Dhabi) the same business? They share a name but no ID number.
  • Are the Alkhayyat brothers the "two co-owners of Power International Holding" the government named? The records fit, but nothing links them directly.
  • Who drafted the 2024 protected-areas amendment, and why?
