Can Technology Expose the Truth?

What can satellites, data and digital records actually establish in Albania?

Technology can make records harder to fake and easier to check. It cannot decide what is legal, establish ownership on its own, or prove corruption. Each tool below is applied to a specific Albanian problem, with its costs, blind spots and risks.

In this chapter
  • Nine technologies, each with a concrete Albanian application.
  • What each can verify, what it cannot, and who could be harmed.
  • A comparison tool: pick two to four technologies and compare them side by side.

The principle

Technology is evidence, not a verdict

It can

Make records tamper-evident and checkable

Signed credentials, ledgers and open data make it harder to change a record quietly.

It can

See what paperwork hides

Satellites revealed the Zvërnec works before any permit was published.

It cannot

Replace law, courts or people
