Questions to bring to class
  1. The resort, the mines and the factories share no owners. Is "country risk" a strong enough tie to treat them as one case? Why or why not?
  2. If a satellite shows a forest cleared at Zvërnec, what else would you need to prove it was illegal? Who should be allowed to make that claim?
  3. Inditex buys Albanian shoes and T-shirts. Under the EU's due-diligence rules, what would it be expected to check, and how would a worker's complaint reach it?
  4. Could a zero-knowledge proof let a factory prove it pays the minimum wage without exposing payroll? What stops a factory from lying in the first place?
  5. Sayari flagged two Abu Dhabi companies with a weak "possibly the same as" match. What harm could AI screening do if people treat fuzzy matches as facts?
  6. Is EU accession a more powerful lever than any supply-chain law here? What does that say about voluntary versus mandatory change?

Key terms

Words you'll need

Country risk

The risks a buyer takes on just by sourcing from a place, such as corruption, weak labour enforcement and opaque ownership. Measured by indices like Transparency International's CPI and the Basel AML Index.

Beneficial owner

The real person who ultimately owns or controls a company, even if someone else's name is on the paperwork as the legal shareholder.

Nominee shareholder

A person listed as owner on behalf of someone else. It is legal in many places, but it can hide who really benefits.

HS code

The Harmonized System number customs uses to classify goods. 2610 is chromium ore; 6403 is leather-upper shoes; 6109 is T-shirts.

Fason

Albanian word for cut-make-trim subcontracting: workshops sew garments and shoe uppers to order for foreign brands.

Verifiable credential and DID

A digitally signed claim, such as "this person owns 50% of this company", that anyone can check without calling the issuer. A DID (decentralised identifier) is the ID it is attached to.

LEI and vLEI

The Legal Entity Identifier is a global 20-character ID for companies. AlbChrome has one. The vLEI is its verifiable-credential version.

Zero-knowledge proof

A way to prove a statement is true, such as "every worker earns at least the minimum wage", without revealing the underlying data.

Mass balance

A traceability method that tracks how much certified material goes in and out, without tracking each physical unit. Common for minerals that get mixed.

Strategic investor status

An Albanian fast-track for big projects. Critics say it is used to push projects through on public or protected land with little scrutiny.

CSRD and CSDDD

The EU's sustainability reporting directive (report your risks) and due-diligence directive (find and fix them), both aimed at large companies.

PEP

Politically exposed person: someone in a prominent public role, screened more closely for corruption risk. It is a category, not an accusation.
