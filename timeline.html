What the timeline shows

Before 2024Long-running problems in the export economy: workers underpaid, miners struggling to organise. Few people outside Albania notice.
2024–2025The law changes, companies are set up in Delaware and Abu Dhabi, and the deal is fast-tracked. Everything is legal on paper, and almost none of it is public.
2026Bulldozers become visible, from the beach and from space. Protest turns a land issue into a referendum on the whole system.
