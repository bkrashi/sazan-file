Conclusion

The answer is not a technology

The Albania story is not proof of wrongdoing; the investigation is ongoing, and many questions remain open. It is proof of something else: when decisions about public land are fast-tracked, ownership is opaque and workers are underpaid, nobody outside the room can check who benefits.

Satellites, registers, open data and supply-chain laws can each narrow that gap. None closes it alone. What closes it is public evidence, institutions able to act on it, and people, in Vlorë, in Tirana and in the brands that buy from Albania, willing to ask who owns the future.
