Before you start

The evidence ladder

Stronger evidence is independent, specific and checkable. Weaker evidence is self-declared, vague or single-sourced.

Official recordA register, statute or customs record. Shows what was declared or decided, not always what is true.
Independent reportingNamed journalists and outlets. Stronger when several outlets confirm independently.
NGO / research findingOften specialised and rigorous, but may advocate a position.
AllegationA claim by one party or under investigation. A lead, not a fact.
UnverifiedA link the records do not confirm, such as a shared name.
Research questionWhere the evidence runs out.

Lessons from the lab

Five rules for reading the Albania story

  1. A name is not an identity.Three companies share the name Atlantic Incubation Partners. Match identifiers, not names.
  2. A register shows declarations, not truth.The Zvërnec land company's paper owner differs from its real owners.
  3. A picture shows change, not legality.Satellites prove the bulldozers came; permits decide whether they should have.
  4. One source is a lead.The reported account freeze rests on a single outlet until confirmed.
  5. Absence of evidence is not evidence of absence.Finding no ownership link for the prime minister doesn't prove there is none, and doesn't prove there is one.
