METHOD & SOURCES

How we know

Every claim on this site traces back to a source on this page.

This investigation combines company and customs records, official statistics and statutes, NGO and union findings, and independent reporting. It distinguishes what is documented from what is reported, alleged or unknown.

Method

Six steps

1 · Records

Company, ownership and risk records were retrieved from Sayari Graph, which aggregates official registers: Delaware, the USPTO, the Abu Dhabi Global Market and Albania's National Business Center, plus third-party risk data. Every person and company named in reporting on the projects was searched.

2 · Trade

Customs shipment records with departure country Albania were grouped by product code and arrival country; the top exporters and importers of chrome ore, leather footwear and T-shirts were examined.

3 · Reporting & NGOs

News, NGO, union and think-tank sources were collected through web research in October–November 2026 and cross-checked where possible.

4 · Status checks

The legal status of laws (CSDDD, CSRD, EUDR, Forced Labour Regulation, Albania's strategic-investment law) and projects (Vlorë airport, Zvërnec works) was checked against sources available in November 2026.

5 · Grading

Every claim is graded by evidence type. Single-source claims are marked as such. Questions the evidence can't answer are listed as research questions rather than filled in.

6 · Separation

Sazan and Zvërnec are kept distinct. The coastal projects and the export supply chains are kept separate, because no record connects them.

LimitsCustoms coverage skews to countries that publish data, and EU destinations are largely missing. Company records lag real changes. Some buyer records are from 2020–2022. The rent figure is a protester's, not a statistic. Map points are approximate and no boundaries are drawn. Euro values for lek amounts are approximate. Ratings of technologies, stakeholders and recommendations are the authors' qualitative judgements. Links to Sayari Graph records require a Sayari account; some news sites block automated link checks but open normally in a browser.
