The Promise of Development

What is being proposed for Albania's coast, and what has actually been approved?

A record tourism boom, a fast-track law for big investors and two luxury proposals in the Bay of Vlorë. Before asking who profits, we need to be precise about what exists: proposals, approvals, permits and works are not the same thing.

In this chapter
  • Sazan Island and Zvërnec are separate projects with different legal histories.
  • Sazan has strategic-investor status (reported); no construction permit was found.
  • Zvërnec saw works on site, with no published permit, until they were suspended in June 2026.
  • More than thirty dated events, each with a source, on one timeline.

The fast-track

The Law on Strategic Investments (2015)

Lets grant with accelerated procedures. The application deadline was extended to December 2027. Critics call it the law "that gave the coast to private individuals".

A change of course

Abolition announced, July 2026

The prime minister said in Brussels the law will be scrapped. A replacement is being drafted; no bill text or vote date has been published.

Elsewhere on the coast

Durrës port and Vlorë airport

Eagle Hills has a Durrës port agreement; the Vlorë airport, inside the Vjosa-Narta landscape, is not operating and its concession is being terminated. Both are separate from Sazan and Zvërnec.
