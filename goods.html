The goods.

Albania doesn't only sell land. It sells chrome for stainless steel, and the labour that sews shoes and T-shirts for global brands. The same weak oversight behind the resort deal follows these goods out of the country, all the way to the shop.

What leaves Albania, and where it lands

Hover a bar for details, or use the chips to highlight one product group.

Read this before you cite the charts. These are shipment records Sayari holds, not Albania's real trade totals. Sayari only sees the importing countries that publish customs data, such as Turkey, China, Indonesia and Mexico. Italy and Germany, Albania's biggest real customers for shoes and clothing, are almost invisible here. The evidence you can see is shaped by who publishes it.
