Summary of findings

What the evidence tells us

T1Ownership is visible on paper but not in practice.Nominees, offshore traders and unnamed factories hide who profits, in the land deal and in both export chains.
T2Rules bend for the powerful and aren't enforced for workers.A law rewritten for a resort, permits skipped, wages and union rights unenforced.
T3Value leaves; pay stays at Europe's floor.€517 minimum wage, public coast fenced off, chrome exported raw.
T4The records stop exactly where accountability would start.No link from Kushner's company to Albanian rights; no mine-level chrome data; no factory-level wage data since 2016.
T5Leverage exists, but it's uneven.EU law reaches the brands; accession and protest reach the government; nothing reaches the investors directly.

Central conclusion: Ethical sourcing from Albania can't be done factory by factory alone. The same governance gaps that let a protected coast be fast-tracked also let wages and union rights go unenforced, so brands and regulators have to act on the system, not just the supplier.

Roadmap

When it could happen

2026
  • Albania repeals or rewrites Law 21/2024 (EU Parliament's deadline: end of 2026)
  • Brands map Albanian suppliers to the factory
  • Open satellite monitoring of Vjosa-Narta
2027
  • Verified beneficial-owner credentials piloted
  • Wage-verification pilots in Albanian workshops
  • EU Forced Labour Regulation applies from December
2028
  • EU due-diligence duties begin to apply to the largest companies (date subject to change)
  • Chrome buyers report mine-level origin
2030
  • Digital product passports phased in for textiles and footwear (expected)
  • Albania's labour standards aligned for EU accession
Method

How these recommendations were made

Each recommendation answers one finding in this file and cites its evidence. Impact, feasibility and cost are qualitative ratings, not modelled numbers. Priority is Urgent where harm is happening now or a deadline is close, High where the gap is documented and the fix is known, and Medium where the fix depends on others acting first.
